# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. No harassment, personal attacks, or discrimination.

Disagree about the code, not the person. Report abuse by opening an issue or, for
anything you'd rather not post publicly, GitHub's
[private vulnerability/abuse reporting](https://github.com/Arrnnnaav/ai-learning-harness/security/advisories/new).

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that don't follow this,
and may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
